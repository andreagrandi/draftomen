---
version: "0.6.6"
date: 2026-11-04
---

- The deck builder now plays 17 lands in every deck. It no longer drops to 16
  lands for an aggressive curve or rises to 18 for a top-heavy one. Winning
  FRA 17Lands decks played 17 lands at every curve, 66% of aggressive decks
  and 78 to 85% of top-heavy ones, and only 1 to 2% played 18. Added
  `experiments/mana-defaults`, which also measured the 7-source floor for each
  main color and the 2-symbol splash limit. Both stay, and the docs now cite
  their source. (#1018)
- Aggressive pools no longer need an `A` grade for a splash card. They use
  the same splash rule as other pools. A third of aggressive winning FRA
  17Lands decks splashed, at the same grades as other decks, and the `A` gate
  would have allowed 1% of those splashes. (#1043)
- Added `experiments/aggressive-splash`, which measures how often aggressive
  and other winning FRA 17Lands decks splash, and the grades of the cards
  they splash. A third of aggressive winning decks splash, at the same grades
  as other decks, and the `A` gate would allow 1% of those splashes. The
  findings propose dropping the gate. No app behavior changes. (#1040)
- Removed the `Splash?` speculative splash label and its 0.85 score
  multiplier. Since splashes can run on basic lands, every splash card has
  enough sources, so the label could no longer appear. The aggressive pool
  setting is now `aggressive_minimum_grade` and still needs an `A`. (#1039)
- Added `experiments/speculative-splash`, which measures when winning FRA
  17Lands drafters took the cards they splashed, their grades, and whether
  off-color picks ended up splashed, in a main color or in the sideboard. The
  findings propose keeping the current pick rule with no grade floor. No app
  behavior changes. (#1028)
- Splash cards no longer need a minimum grade. A splash card goes in when it
  beats the card it replaces, and a third-color card sets the splash color
  when its 17Lands win rate beats the weakest of the pool's best 23 on-pair
  spells. Aggressive pools still need an `A`. Of the cards that winning FRA
  17Lands decks splashed, only 17% (Premier) and 14% (Pick Two) were `A-` or
  better, and the most common grade was `C+`. (#1027)
- Splashes can now run on basic lands. A splash covers one color and up to 3
  cards. A card with one symbol of the splash color needs 3 land sources, and
  a card with two symbols needs 4. Basics of the splash color fill whatever
  the drafted dual lands do not cover, so a splash no longer needs 2 duals.
  The pick overlay and the build sheet use the same rule. In winning 17Lands
  splash decks, 14 to 22% had no dual land for the splash, and 70% of decks
  splashing a two-symbol card had 4 or more splash sources. (#1026)
- The deck builder now gives a color at least 6 land sources when a spell in
  the deck has two or more symbols of that color, such as `{1}{B}{B}`. When
  the lands cannot reach 6, the builder cuts the card and picks a
  replacement. The build sheet names the card and says whether the builder
  added basics or cut it. About 85% of such cards in winning three-color
  17Lands decks had 6 or more sources of their color. (#1025)
- The deck builder now gives each color of a three-color deck at least 4
  land sources when it splits the basics. Dual lands count toward each color
  they make, and non-land fixers do not count. The lightest color of a
  winning three-color deck had 4 or more land sources in about 90% of 17Lands
  decks. Two-color mana bases are unchanged. (#1024)
- A three-color deck is now a triad candidate only when each of its colors
  appears on at least 4 of the top 23 playable spells. A color with 1 to 3
  cards is a splash, so splash rules apply instead. 17Lands labels a third
  color with 1 to 3 cards a splash, and in 98% of three-color decks the
  lightest color has 4 or more cards. The deck builder and the pick engine
  both use the new count, and the pick engine shows it in the blocked reason,
  such as "green has 3 cards, triads need 4". The `triad_min_color_share`
  setting is gone. (#1023)
- The deck builder no longer needs three fixing pieces in the pool before it
  scores a three-color deck. Every balanced three-color deck is scored, and
  its fixing adjustment decides how much the missing fixing costs. The
  `triad_min_fixing_sources` setting is gone. (#1022)
- The deck builder now scores a three-color deck by its fixing pieces. It
  moves the 17Lands win rate of the deck by the measured gap for that piece
  count, from -18.5 points with none to +2.0 points with six or more. Two-color
  decks are unchanged, and the build sheet prints the count and the adjustment
  for each three-color deck. (#1021)
- The deck builder now sorts pool cards into land sources, non-land fixers
  and cards that do not fix. A three-color deck counts basic landcycling
  cards, any-color mana rocks and Treasure makers as fixing. It no longer
  counts Konstrari Improviser, whose token needs a second spell before it
  makes mana. (#1020)
- Add `scripts/measure_deck_rules.py`, which reads a local 17Lands game data
  file and prints the color and mana stats of winning decks, such as dual land
  counts, sources for double-symbol cards and splash card grades. It uses
  cached files only and never opens a network connection. (#1019)
- The pool summary creature and removal counts no longer jump between picks.
  After a pick they used to count every color until the next pack was scored,
  then drop back to the likely pair. They now keep counting against the last
  pair, and the GUI and TUI labels show it, such as `Creatures (WU)`, or
  `Creatures (any color)` before a pair is inferred.
- Native bundle builds now pin Python 3.14 on macOS and 3.12 on Windows, so
  a runner image that ships a newer Python cannot break the Nuitka build.
- The deck builder no longer treats a hybrid card that the pair can cast, such
  as {R/W} in a white deck, as a splash card. These cards used to sit on the
  bench with `cut: splash cap` when no splash was active. (#998)
- Suggested decks now play 15 to 17 creatures, up from a floor of 14, and
  17Lands trophy averages can no longer pull the target below 15. A pool with
  fewer castable creatures plays all of them instead of dropping the creature
  floor to 0, and the build shows a warning such as
  `Only 13 creatures available in WU`. (#999)
- The session pool state now carries land, creature and removal counts and a
  creature pace projection, and the build result carries a removal count, so
  frontends can show them. Creature and removal counts include only cards the
  likely pair can cast, and temporary taps do not count as removal. (#1000)
- The pick engine has a creature pace term. After pack 1, when the pool
  projects below 15 castable creatures, castable creatures get up to 2 extra
  points and a reason such as
  `creature needed, 9 so far, on pace for 13 of 15`. In a backtest on 800 FRA
  and 800 HOB trophy drafts, top-1 agreement was unchanged and more
  engine-drafted pools reached 15 creatures, so the term is on by default.
  (#1003)
- GUI Settings and the TUI settings have a "Creature pace bonus" switch, on by
  default and saved with the other preferences. Turning it off removes the
  creature pace reason and score change from the current recommendations. The
  pool summary still shows the creature pace line. (#1004)
- The GUI draft view now shows lands, creatures, removal and creature pace in
  the pool summary, and the build page shows the removal count in both
  layouts. (#1001)
- The TUI pool summary now shows lands, creatures, removal and creature pace
  against the 15 to 17 target, and the TUI build summary shows the removal
  count next to creatures and lands. (#1002)
- The GUI Live Draft focused intel panel now shows a "Why this score" section
  with the pick explanation, including the creature pace line when that
  setting is on. (#1013)
- A deck builder test now checks that a WU pool with 12 WU creatures and 3
  hybrid creatures castable in WU builds a deck with at least 15 creatures,
  all 3 hybrids and no splash. (#1012)
